## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a vibrant, workshop-driven academic atmosphere centered on youth co-creation, ecological awareness, and civic stewardship. It pairs institutional educational credibility with the tactile, punchy spontaneity of participatory Design Thinking sessions.

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
- **Collaborative & Empathetic:** The interface elevates student agency, field interviews, empathy maps, and collaborative workshop artifacts rather than top-down institutional mandates.
- **Energetic & Rigorous:** Balances the structure of formal design pedagogy with lively, high-character display forms that celebrate iterative experimentation.
- **Civic & Organic:** Rooted in physical space stewardship, community care, and green campus transformations.

### Visual Style
A hybrid of **Experimental Editorial** and **Tactile Workshop Lab**. It merges hyper-legible body typography with idiosyncratic, kinetic display headings, crisp structural cards, and high-contrast badges representing the 5 Design Thinking phases (Empatizar, Definir, Idear, Prototipar, Testear).

## Colors

The color palette anchors community ecology and student agency against a clean, architectural backdrop.

### Palette Roles & Balance
- **Primary (`#10b981` Emerald Green):** The core life-force token. Designates ecological renewal, stewardship success metrics, primary interactive elements, and proactive field findings. Deep emerald accents (`#059669`) serve as interactive hover/pressed states.
- **Secondary (`#0f172a` Deep Slate/Navy):** The architectural bedrock. Provides high-contrast anchor weights, structured navigation frames, primary headlines, and executive summaries.
- **Tertiary (`#f59e0b` Warm Amber):** The ideation spark. Used for the "Idear" phase, sticky-note callouts, student quotes, workshop insights, and pending hypotheses.
- **Neutral (`#64748b` Slate Gray) & Background Tiers:**
  - Page canvas: `#f8fafc` (Slate 50) for a soft paper-like academic canvas.
  - Card/Surface level: `#ffffff` (Pure White) for crisp separation.
  - Surface subtle: `#f1f5f9` (Slate 100) for structured meta-blocks, table headers, and inactive timeline steps.
  - Border low-contrast: `#e2e8f0` (Slate 200).
  - High-emphasis body text: `#1e293b` (Slate 800).
  - Low-emphasis captions: `#64748b` (Slate 500).

### Color Application Rules
- Interactive green must maintain WCAG AAA compliance when paired with `#0f172a` or `#ffffff`.
- The 5 Design Thinking stages are color-coded with specific accent pairings:
  - *Empatizar:* Deep Teal / Slate (`#0f172a` with `#10b981` border)
  - *Definir:* Slate Navy (`#1e293b`)
  - *Idear:* Warm Amber (`#f59e0b`)
  - *Prototipar:* Bright Emerald (`#10b981`)
  - *Testear:* Coral / Indigo hybrid (`#6366f1`)

## Typography

The pairing creates an immediate conversational balance between expressive student dialogue and hyper-structured readability.

- **Headlines (Bricolage Grotesque):** Expressive, experimental, human, and kinetic. Its distinctive ink-traps and lively curves evoke the dynamism of workshop chalkboards, posters, and collaborative sprints.
- **Body & Labels (Lexend):** Designed explicitly for optimal reading rhythm and visual accessibility. Guarantees zero cognitive fatigue when reviewing student interview transcripts, survey percentages, and problem statements.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- `display-hero` is reserved for portfolio project titles and stage transition hero markers.
- Never use Bricolage Grotesque in long paragraphs; it is dedicated strictly to `headline-*` roles.
- `label-md` utilizes uppercase tracking for method step indicators (e.g., `FASE 01 / EMPATIZAR`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a responsive 12-column grid system built on an 8pt architectural rhythm, accommodating both editorial storytelling and multi-artifact workshop galleries.

### Grid Anatomy & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥1024px):** 12-column layout with 24px (`space-lg`) gutters and a max container width of `76rem` (1216px). Section padding expands to `space-4xl` to allow deep visual pacing.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px page margins.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`space-md`) gutters and 16px page margins.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Persistent Stage Tracker:** A top-pinned or floating horizontal island housing the 5 Design Thinking stages. Reflows into a compressed pill navigation with icon/step counters on mobile viewports.
- **Workshop Asymmetry:** Cards containing student evidence (photos, whiteboard stickies, metric charts) use staggered multi-column spans (e.g., 7 columns for qualitative narrative, 5 columns for quantitative evidence).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of crisp architectural layering, soft ambient halos, and precise micro-borders.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#f8fafc` canvas. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Canvases):** Pure white background (`#ffffff`), `1px solid #e2e8f0`, supported by a subtle dual ambient shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05);`
- **Level 2 (Hover State & Interactive Artifacts):** Lifted cards (interview highlights, prototype blueprints) receive:
  - `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
  - Border transitions to `#cbd5e1` with a subtle `-2px` transform on the Y axis.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Nav & Floating Modals):** Frosted glass effect using 85% opacity `#ffffff` with a `12px` backdrop-filter blur and subtle perimeter shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1);`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.

### Environmental Accent Glow
Key milestone cards (such as the chosen final prototype) apply a faint, diffused ambient glow derived from the emerald primary seed:
- `box-shadow: 0 12px 30px -8px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.18);`

## Shapes

With a roundedness level of `3` (Pill-shaped / friendly modernism), geometry is soft, tactile, and highly approachable, evoking contemporary educational design labs.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Micro / Chips / Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`, 9999px border-radius) for phase labels, tag taxonomy, and status indicators.
- **Buttons & Form Fields:** Fully rounded pill curves (height 44px–48px with 9999px radius or generous `1.5rem` minimum).
- **Cards & Surfaces:** `1.5rem` (`rounded-2xl`) outer perimeter with `1rem` (`rounded-xl`) internal child container nests.
- **Sticky Navigation Container:** Fully enclosed pill shape floating comfortably within the viewport.

## Components

### 1. Sticky 5-Stage Navigation Tracker
- **Anatomy:** Floating horizontal pill centered at the top/bottom of the viewport.
- **Elements:** 5 interconnected nodes corresponding to:
  1. *Empatizar*
  2. *Definir*
  3. *Idear*
  4. *Prototipar*
  5. *Testear*
- **States:** Active phase is rendered in primary emerald `#10b981` (or phase accent) with an inverted white label and glowing sub-dot; completed phases feature slate checkmarks; future phases remain muted `#94a3b8`.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#10b981` emerald fill, white text, bold pill silhouette (`rounded-full`). Hover shifts to `#059669` with an upward transition of `1px`.
- **Secondary Action:** Dark slate background `#0f172a`, crisp white typography, pill-shaped.
- **Outline / Ghost Action:** Border `1.5px solid #e2e8f0`, transparent fill, text `#1e293b`. Hover activates subtle emerald-tinted wash `#ecfdf5`.

### 3. Methodology Cards & Sticky Notes
- **Standard Insight Card:** Level 1 elevation, white surface, `1.5rem` rounded corners, thin `1px solid #e2e8f0` stroke. Contains stage tag, headline, evidence body, and student attribution.
- **Ideation Sticky Note Component:** Tactile accent card rendered in soft amber `#fef3c7` with amber border `#fde68a`, slightly rotated (`-1deg` or `+1deg`) to mimic co-design brainstorm sessions.

### 4. Chips & Tags
- **Stage Pill:** Height 28px, uppercase `label-md` Lexend typography, `padding: 0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Metric Pill:** Slate dark pill displaying quantifiable field data (e.g., `-65% basuras`, `120 entrevistas`).

### 5. Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Inputs:** Height 48px, rounded pill border (`rounded-full`), padded `1.25rem` horizontally. Border `#cbd5e1`, focusing to `#10b981` with a `3px` focus ring tinted at `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Emerald active state, smooth `0.2s` spring transitions, fully rounded check targets.

### 6. Quotes & Testimonials
- Academic quotes from San Fernando students and teachers are styled with an emerald vertical accent bar (`3px`), italicized Lexend body, and a pill avatar badge of the participant.
